About the Shop

A trusted fixture in the Adams Avenue business district

Charles Alteration & Tailoring has been a neighborhood tailoring institution on Adams Avenue for over 20 years, handling everything from simple hems and zipper repairs to full custom tailoring for men, women, and kids.

The shop also offers 3-day dry cleaning, laundry, and pressing. Regulars describe the staff as friendly and meticulous, they measure and adjust garments on the spot to make sure the fit is exactly right before finalizing any alteration.

With a 4-star rating built on 120+ reviews, it has earned a reputation as a trustworthy, reasonably-priced neighborhood gem in the Normal Heights / University Heights area.
